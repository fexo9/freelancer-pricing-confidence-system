## What it is

An 11-language pricing decision kit for freelancers: a floor-rate
calculator, value tiers, a quote checklist, a discount guard, and
objection scripts — in English, Mandarin (Simplified), French, Spanish,
Arabic (RTL), Russian, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Hindi, and Italian.
Same 5 sheets, same formulas, full parity. No macros.

- **Rate Calculator** — monthly costs + target income + billable hours +
  utilization = your FLOOR hourly rate, error-guarded.
- **Value Tiers** — floor / target / premium rates (×1.0 / ×1.5 / ×2.0),
  project price estimator, quick rate-safety check.
- **Quote Checklist** — 10 scored checkpoints + 4 red-flag signals =
  GREEN / AMBER / RED verdict before you send any quote.
- **Discount Guard** — enter the requested discount, get the effective
  rate vs. your floor: safe / conditional / walk-away. Anchor rule:
  never discount the rate — discount the scope.
- **Objection Scripts** — 5 copy-paste replies for "too expensive"
  pushback.

Plus: quick-start guides (EN + AR), a worked example (EN + AR, fictional
data), FAQ (EN + AR), limits notes, and a file-by-file inventory.
45 files total.

## What it does NOT do

- Does NOT guarantee clients will accept your rate.
- Does NOT set your market price (multipliers are editable starting
  suggestions).
- Does NOT give tax, legal, or accounting advice.
- Does NOT track invoices, expenses, or payments.
- Does NOT include market-rate data.
- Does NOT model fees or platform commissions.
- Does NOT sync between the 11 language workbooks (11 separate copies).
